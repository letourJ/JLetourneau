About Me

GIS Student | Spatial Analyst | PC Builder

Me Currently

Hi! I'm Jordan Letourneau, I’m a GIS student at BCIT with a Bachelor’s in Political Science.

Outside of geospatial work, I’m a tech enthusiast: I run a personal server, build PCs, and enjoy tinkering with Linux systems. When I’m not at a computer, you’ll find me cycling or spending time with my partner and our little one.

I'll be finishing up BCIT's Advanced Diploma in GIS early 2027, and I'm currently looking for a workplace or project sponsor for January-April 2027.

Education & Skills

BCIT Advanced GIS Diploma
Coursework includes: Spatial Analysis, Web GIS, Cartography, Remote Sensing, Database Management, and Programming for GIS.

Technical Skills
ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, Python, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, Bootstrap, PostgreSQL, Linux, FME, Leaflet, Mapbox

Professional Interests

My interest in GIS comes from having a curiosity about the ways we map ourselves, a life long habit of poking around under the proverbial hood of computers, and of course a love of maps themselves. I'm very interested in working with/on transit, and using GIS to help make everyday life more accessible and equitable.
